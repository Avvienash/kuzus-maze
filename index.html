Kuzu's Maze

A calm memory puzzle. Step across face-down wooden tiles, collect five flowers in order, and remember where the vine monsters hide.

Coming soon toGoogle Play Coming later to theApp Store
  • Play solo, or pass one phone between up to five players.
  • A new maze every game, plus a Hard Memory Mode where tiles flip back as you leave them.
  • Fully offline. No accounts, no sign-in.
  • Ads only between games, never during play. One small purchase removes them.

Based on the board game Kuzu's Maze by Stellar Factory.
