Support

Found a bug, have an idea, or stuck on something? Email avvienash@gmail.com. It helps to mention your device model and whether you're on Android or iOS.

Common questions

How do I play?

Open How to Play from the main menu, or play the short tutorial. It takes about two minutes.

I bought Remove Ads but I'm seeing ads

On Android, the purchase is restored automatically when the app starts, as long as you're signed in to the same Google account. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings → Restore Purchases. If that doesn't work, email us with your store receipt.

How do I change my ad consent choice?

Open Settings → Privacy Options. This option appears in regions where consent is required. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I reset my statistics?

Yes: Settings → Reset Statistics. This can't be undone.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. The game works fully offline. The internet is only used to load ads.

Is my progress backed up?

No. There are no accounts or cloud saves, so settings and statistics stay on your device and are removed if you uninstall the app.
