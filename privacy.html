Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Kuzu's Maze mobile game (“the app”) uses, for Android and iOS. In short: the app collects no personal data itself. The only data that leaves your device is what our ad provider, Google AdMob, collects to show ads, and only within the choices you make.

What the app keeps on your device

The app stores these on your device only:

  • Your settings (sound, music, haptics and display options).
  • Player names and colours you enter for pass-and-play games.
  • Your statistics, and any game in progress.
  • Whether you have bought Remove Ads, and counters used to space out ads.

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. We have no servers, no accounts and no analytics. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. You can also reset your statistics in Settings.

Advertising

The free version shows occasional full-screen ads between games, provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID or, on iOS, the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking.
  • Your IP address and the approximate location derived from it.
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system and language.
  • How you interact with ads, plus diagnostic data about ad performance.

Google handles this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. We don't receive this data in a form that identifies you.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where it's required): on first launch the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy Options.
  • iOS tracking: the app asks Apple's tracking permission once. If you decline, you still see ads, but they aren't personalised. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Google or Privacy settings.
  • Remove Ads: a one-time purchase turns off all ads, and with them all ad-related data collection.

Purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation that the purchase was made, and caches that on your device so it works offline.

Children

The app is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone, children included. If you believe a child's data has been collected through ads in the app, contact us and we'll help you reach the right party.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data collected by Google for advertising, you can use the choices above or Google's tools at My Ad Center. You're welcome to contact us with any question.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics or trackers, and its fonts are served from the site itself. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.
